Scientific Lineage & Professional History

The foundation of Kusmus Smart Tech was laid not in a boardroom, but in the rigorous corridors of theoretical physics. With more than two decades of academic excellence, I have transformed my teaching and research in Computational Science and engineering using Python into a Smart Tech venture, evolving from diverse training to versatile consultancy.

As a pioneer of Python in Africa, I was the Initiator/Coordinator of the Python African Computational Science and Engineering Tour Project (PACSETPro), dedicated to empowering the next generation of African computational scientists for years.

The SSLP Initiative

To boost accessibility and inclusivity, I initiated the Smartphones for Smart Learning Project (SSLP). In this framework, smartphones are adopted as mobile computation laboratories, scalable via cloud computing into mobile supercomputers in our pockets.

This initiative is key to driving the requirements of the 4th Sustainable Development Goal (SDG4) on education, ensuring that sovereign intelligence is accessible to all, regardless of their hardware constraints.
